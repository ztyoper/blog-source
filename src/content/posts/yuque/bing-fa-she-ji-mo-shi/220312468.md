3. concurrent-design-patterns-guarded-suspension：保护性暂挂模式案例源码

4. concurrent-design-patterns-two-phase-termination：两阶段终止模式案例源码

5. concurrent-design-patterns-promise：承诺模式案例源码

6. concurrent-design-patterns-producer-comsumer：生产者消费者模式案例源码

7. concurrent-design-patterns-active-object：主动对象模式案例源码

8. concurrent-design-patterns-thread-pool：线程池模式案例源码

9. concurrent-design-patterns-threadlocal：线程特有存储模式案例源码

10. concurrent-design-patterns-thread-close：串行线程封闭模式案例源码

11. concurrent-design-patterns-master-slave：主仆模式案例源码

12. concurrent-design-patterns-pipeline-framework：流水线通用框架型源码

13. concurrent-design-patterns-pipeline：流水线模式案例源码

14. concurrent-design-patterns-half-sync-async：半同步半异步模式案例源码
